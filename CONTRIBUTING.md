# Contributing

Thanks for taking an interest. Contributions are welcome: extension list updates, hotkey
corrections and guide improvements.

## What belongs here

- An extension slug that is wrong or unpublished
- A hotkey that collides with another or targets a command that does not exist
- Improvements to the guides
- Improvements to the guides

## What does not belong here

- A hotkey or extension that only reflects one person's taste rather than something broadly
  useful, keep that in your own copy

## How to contribute

1. Fork the repository and create a branch named `fix/<short-description>` or
   `feat/<short-description>`.
2. Make your change. Check a hotkey's command against the extension's own commands in Raycast
   first. Keep every letter within a modifier group unique.
3. Open a pull request with a clear title and a one-paragraph description of what changed and
   why. CI checks the extension list format.

## Style rules

> [!IMPORTANT]
> - **Comments**: explain the why, not the what.
> - **UK English** in prose and documentation.

## Reporting bugs

Open an issue with your Raycast version and platform, what you expected versus what happened.
